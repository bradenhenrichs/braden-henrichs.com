01 · About

A little more context

I'm a product leader based in Dublin, with a career that started in project management at Epic before moving fully into product at Oneview Healthcare, eShopWorld, Wayflyer, and Cubic Telecom. I've owned P&L, run product teams, and stayed hands-on as an individual contributor — often at the same time.

The common thread has been turning ambiguous, 0-to-1 problems into products with real commercial traction, then building the team around them. Outside of work, I'm usually sea swimming, climbing, or hiking somewhere around Dublin.
